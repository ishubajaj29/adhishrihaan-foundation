What We Do

Our Pillars of Support

Capacity Building

Care in Action: We offer tailored training programs, essential funding assistance, and critical technological support to enhance the operational efficiency and program effectiveness of our partner NGOs. This includes workshops on project management, financial literacy, fundraising strategies, and digital tools.

Visibility

Care in Action: We are dedicated to amplifying the impactful stories of these silent warriors. We help them effectively communicate their achievements, document their journeys, and gain recognition through diverse channels.

Networking

Care in Action: We connect grassroots NGOs with each other, fostering collaborative learning and shared solutions. We also link them with larger philanthropic organizations, corporate CSR initiatives, academic institutions, and government bodies.

The Shrihaan Sahyog Grant

As a testament to our unwavering commitment to direct and impactful social initiatives, the Shrihaan Sahyog Grant provides crucial support to grassroots organizations.

The grant offers funding and guidance to small, community-rooted NGOs so they can strengthen programs, reach more people and build a foundation for long-term impact.
